What the ERP work involves

Finance
Cash-flow reporting, accounting workflows and financial data processing
Procurement

Engineering of the system
Python business logic, XML views, access controls and SQL — designed around client-specific workflows
Operations
Module deployments and upgrades, production issue resolution, investigation of data inconsistencies across business processes and database records

Engineering judgement under constraints

Reproducibility
Quality control held to written procedures, because a precursor is only comparable if the process that made it was identical
Traceability
Drawings, layouts and study assumptions documented so that later decisions can be traced back to their basis
Honest reporting
Prototypes reported as prototypes; feasibility studies reported with their assumptions visible

Confidentiality. Client names, plant data, drawings, screenshots and financial figures from consultancy and industry work are deliberately omitted.
